# Hybrid Transfer Rubric v0.1.0

## General
Single-capability stimulus with no meaningful sport constraint.

## Specific
Sport-relevant movement/task is trained, but state carry-over into the next demand is limited or intentionally reset.

## Hybrid
At least two meaningful demands are linked by continuous or limited recovery, and the earlier demand changes the subsequent performance state.

## Performance
The task reproduces the competition-relevant combination closely enough that performance is the primary objective rather than merely creating an adaptation stimulus.

## Practical test
Ask three questions:
1. Are there at least two meaningful demands?
2. Is recovery limited/continuous?
3. Does the earlier demand materially affect the later task?

Three yes answers support `hybrid`. If #3 is no, do not label it Hybrid merely because two exercises appear in the same workout.
